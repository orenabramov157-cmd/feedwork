Pricing & Demo

Priced around your brand, not a rate card.

There is no menu of packages here, because a five-product candle brand and a forty-product outdoor catalog do not need the same work. Tell us what you make, and the quote is built around exactly that. You get the number before anything starts, and it does not move.

01How a quote is built

Four things decide the number.

Every quote comes from the same four questions. Answer them in one email and the number comes back the same day.

01 — Your catalog

How many products

One hero product needs a tight set of angles. A full catalog needs coverage. The size of the batch follows the size of the shelf.

02 — Your formats

Static, video, or both

Static ads are the fastest and cheapest way to find what sells. Video costs more to produce, so it usually comes second, built on the angle the statics already proved.

03 — Your cadence

One batch or a rhythm

Some brands need one strong launch set. Others want fresh creative landing every month so ads never go stale. A standing rhythm prices differently than a one-off.

04 — Your starting point

What you already have

Clean product photos shorten the job. A single phone photo is fine too, it just changes where the work starts. Either way, nothing is reshot that does not need to be.

02Every project

What every quote includes, no matter the size.

Built from your product

Your actual thing

Everything is generated from your photography, so the label, the finish and the shape in the ad are yours, not a lookalike's.

Written from your buyers

Copy in their words

Your reviews get read before a single headline is written. The ads say what your customers already say.

Sized for the platform

Feed and story, ready to upload

Delivered in the sizes Meta actually wants. You or whoever runs your ads uploads them. Nobody here needs a login.

48 hours

On time, or you hear first

Two days from photo to finished files. If a batch will be late you get told before the deadline, not after.

Until it is right

Revisions included

If the thing in the ad does not look like the thing you sell, it is not finished. Changes get made until it does.
