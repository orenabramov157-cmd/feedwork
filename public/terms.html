Quotes and scope

Every project starts with a written quote that says what is included, the price and when it will be ready. The quote, as shown in your client portal, is the scope of the project. Nothing starts until you approve it. Work outside that scope gets its own quote.

Payment

Prices are fixed and in US dollars. Invoices are paid through the client portal by card or bank payment, processed by Stripe. The payment terms for your project, such as a deposit and a final payment, are written in your quote.

Refunds

To ask for a refund, email consult@feedworkstudio.com. Approved refunds go back to the original card through Stripe, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
