Privacy policy

How Feedwork Studio collects, uses and shares personal information through feedworkstudio.com, the client portal at portal.feedworkstudio.com, our emails and our project work (together, the "Service").

Who we are

Feedwork Studio ("we", "us") is a sole proprietorship based in California that makes ad creative and websites for businesses. Contact us at consult@feedworkstudio.com or +1 972-742-2543.

Personal information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Contact data: your name, company, email, phone number, location and website.
  • Project data: what you sell, what you need, your notes, and the files, logos, photos and product details you send us.
  • Communications data: messages, change requests and approvals in the portal, and anything you send us by email, phone or text.
  • Transaction data: invoices, amounts and payment status. Card and bank details are collected by Stripe, not by us; we never see or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically:

  • Device data: browser and device type, operating system, IP address, time zone and general location such as city or region.
  • Online activity data: pages you view, how long you stay, the site you came from, clicks and scrolling, and, at times, a recording of how a page was used. We collect this with PostHog analytics.
  • Email interaction data: links in our emails can carry a short code that tells us which company the email was sent to and whether the link was opened.
  • Cookies and similar storage: the portal uses a cookie to keep you signed in, and the site stores a small value in your browser to remember the email code above.

Information from other sources: when we research a business before contacting it, we use public sources such as its website and public business listings.

How we use it

  • To run the Service: answer your request, write your quote, do the work, run the portal, take payment and send updates.
  • To improve it: understand which pages are useful and fix what is not.
  • To market our business: contact businesses that may want our work. You can opt out at any time, as described below.
  • To comply and protect: meet legal, tax and accounting duties, respond to lawful requests, enforce our terms, and prevent spam, fraud and abuse.
  • To make de-identified data: we may turn information into aggregate or de-identified data that no longer identifies you, and we will not try to re-identify it.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we do not train our own AI models on your files or project content.

How we share it

  • Service providers that process information only to run the Service for us: Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Telegram (a notice of new requests to Oren's phone), Resend (portal email), Calendly (call booking), PostHog (analytics), Google Fonts (typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads), and the AI image and video tools we use to make your work.
  • Payment processor: Stripe processes payments under its own privacy policy.
  • Professional advisors such as lawyers and accountants, when they work for us.
  • Authorities and others, when we believe in good faith it is required by law or needed to protect rights, safety or property.
  • A buyer or successor, if the business is ever sold or transferred.
  • People you tell us to share with, such as a teammate you add to your portal project.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information as long as we need it for the purposes above, including legal, tax and accounting duties and resolving disputes. Requests are kept while we are talking with you about work; project records are kept for the project and as long as tax rules require afterward. When we no longer need information, we delete it or de-identify it.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction and deletion: email consult@feedworkstudio.com and we will show you, correct or delete what we hold, except what the law requires us to keep. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.
  • Marketing emails: reply "stop" or use the opt-out in the email and we will stop within 10 business days. Project and payment emails continue while you are a client.
  • Cookies and analytics: you can block cookies and analytics in your browser or with an extension. The site works without them; the portal needs its sign-in cookie.
  • Do Not Track: we do not currently respond to browser Do Not Track signals.
  • Declining to give information: we need contact and project details to quote and do work; without them we may not be able to help.
  • State privacy laws: if a state privacy law applies to us and gives you more rights, contact us and we will honor them. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

Security

We use technical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encrypted connections, sign-in by one-time email code, and project files that only your project team and Feedwork can open. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee it.

Other sites and international processing

Our pages link to other sites, such as Calendly and Stripe, that have their own privacy policies. We are based in the United States, and some service providers may process information in other countries where privacy laws may be less protective than yours.

Children

The Service is for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. If we make material changes we will change the date at the top and post the new version here.
