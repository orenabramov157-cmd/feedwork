Privacy policy

What we collect when you use feedworkstudio.com and the client portal at portal.feedworkstudio.com, why, and who else touches it.

Who we are

Feedwork Studio is a sole proprietorship based in California. You can reach us at consult@feedworkstudio.com or +1 972-742-2543.

What we collect

  • When you send a project request: your company, name, email, phone number, location, website, what you sell, what you need, and any notes you write. We also keep your time zone and the network (IP) address the request came from, which we use to stop spam and abuse.
  • When you book a call: booking runs through Calendly, which collects the details you give it under its own privacy policy.
  • When you use the client portal: your name and email, the files you upload, your messages, change requests and approvals, and a record of invoices and payments. A cookie keeps you signed in. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • When you browse the site: we use PostHog analytics to see which pages are visited and how they are used, including your browser and device type and, at times, a recording of clicks and scrolling on the page. If you arrive from a link in one of our emails, the link carries a short code that tells us which company the email went to.

How we use it

To answer your request, write a quote, do the work, send you updates, take payment, keep the site and portal secure, and understand what parts of the site are useful. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own advertising.

Who else handles it

We use a small set of service providers who process information only to run these services for us:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and portal and stores requests and project files.
  • Telegram carries a notice of each new request to Oren's phone.
  • Resend sends portal emails such as sign-in codes and updates.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Calendly handles call booking.
  • PostHog provides site analytics.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on the site, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Requests are kept while we are talking with you about work. Portal records are kept for the length of the project and as long as tax and accounting rules require afterward. Ask us and we will delete what we are not required to keep.

Your choices

Email consult@feedworkstudio.com to see, correct or delete your information. To stop emails from us, reply and say so, or use the opt-out in the email, and we will stop. You can block cookies and analytics in your browser settings; the site works without them, though the portal needs its sign-in cookie.

Do Not Track

Our site does not currently change its behavior in response to browser Do Not Track signals. You can still block analytics with your browser or an extension.

Children

This site is for businesses. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
