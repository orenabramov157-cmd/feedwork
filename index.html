03How it's built

Built around your brand, not a template.

Nothing here is generic stock work with a logo dropped on it. Every batch starts from your product and the way your own customers already talk about it.

Your product

Your actual thing

Built from your own photography, so the packaging, the finish, the label and the shape are yours rather than something that looks close enough.

Your customers

Their words

Your reviews get read before anything is written. The copy uses the words your buyers already use, not the words a marketer would reach for.

Free shipping over a threshold, a bundle, a seasonal promo. Whatever is live goes in the creative instead of a generic call to action.

04Questions

The things people ask on the phone

Do you need access to my ad account?

No, and it is not offered. Feedwork delivers files. You or whoever runs your ads uploads them. Nobody here needs a login, partner access, or admin on anything.

Do you run the ads too?

No. Media buying is a different job and it is not this one. If you already have someone running your ads, they get more to work with. If you do not, the files still work whenever you start.

What if it does not look like my product?

Then it gets rebuilt. Product accuracy is the whole job, so if the thing in the ad does not look like the thing you sell, it is not finished.

What do you actually need from me?

A photo of the product and the name of it. If you have a current offer or a discount code worth putting in the ad, that helps. Nothing else, and there is no form to fill out.

Will you promise these will perform?

No. Anyone who promises you ad performance without controlling your product, your price, your landing page and your budget is guessing. Feedwork promises finished files, on time, that look like your product. That is the part that can actually be guaranteed.

How fast, really?

48 hours from the photo arriving. If a batch is going to be late you get told before the deadline, not after.
